## 1. Schedule H / H1 / X dispensing

- **Schedule H, H1, X** drugs may be sold by retail only on and in accordance with the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner — **rule 65(9)(a)**.
- **Valid prescription — rule 65(10):** (a) in writing, signed with usual signature, dated; (b) states **patient name and address** (or animal owner); (c) states **total amount of medicine and dose**. The prescriber's registration number is **not** required by 65(10) — the registers require the prescriber's **name and address** only.
- **Dispensing duties — rule 65(11):** no dispensing more than once unless the prescriber says so; at dispensing, note **on the prescription** (above the prescriber's signature) the **seller's name and address and the dispensing date**.
- **No substitution — rule 65(11A):** a preparation cannot be supplied in lieu of the prescribed one.
- **Labelling — rule 97(1):** Schedule H = "Rx" + caution legend; H1 = "Rx" (NRx if NDPS) + legend; X = "XRx". Expiry printed as month/year, valid to last day of month.
- **PPR 2015 (professional):** reg. 2(j) prescription content definition (incl. prescriber registration number — stricter than central rules); reg. 4.3 dispense only against RMP prescription, no substitution; **reg. 6.2 prescription records kept 5 years** by the registered pharmacist; copies within 72 hours on request.

## 2. Prescription register and Schedule H1 register

- **Prescription register — rule 65(3)(1)(a)–(g):** serial number; date of supply; **prescriber name and address**; **patient name and address**; drug name and quantity; **for Schedule C, H, H1 — manufacturer name, batch number, expiry date of potency**; signature of the Registered Pharmacist supervising. Recorded **at the time of supply**; the register serial number is entered on the prescription.
  - Provisos: a serially-numbered cash/credit memo book may substitute for original-container, non-compounded drugs; repeat dispensing gets a cross-referenced simplified entry.
- **Schedule H1 separate register — rule 65(3)(1)(h)** (inserted by G.S.R. 588(E) 30.08.2013, w.e.f. 27.02.2014): name and address of prescriber, name of patient, name of drug, quantity supplied; recorded at time of supply; **retained for three years**; open for inspection. (The register does not require the prescriber's registration number.)
- **Retention — rule 65(7):** registers/records kept **not less than 2 years** from last entry, except where otherwise provided (H1 register: **3 years**; wholesale sale-memo carbon copies: 3 years; Schedule X prescription copies and signed orders: 2 years).

## 3. Schedule X specifics

- Prescription **in duplicate**, licensee retains one copy **2 years** — rule 65(9)(a). Supply to RMPs/hospitals against **signed written order**, preserved 2 years — 65(9)(b).
- **Bound, page-numbered register with separate pages per drug — rule 65(21)(a)**; fields (65(21)(b)): date; quantity received + supplier name/address + supplier licence no.; drug name; quantity supplied; manufacturer; batch/lot no.; patient/purchaser name+address; **prescription reference number**; bill no./date; supervisor signature.
- **Storage under lock and key — rule 65(12)** in a cupboard/drawer reserved solely for Schedule X.
- Separate licences: retail **Form 20F**, wholesale **Form 20G** (rule 61(3)); application Form 19C (59(2)).

## 4. Licences and qualified persons

| Form | Rule | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Form 20 | 61(1) | Retail sale, drugs other than Schedules C, C(1), X |
| Form 20A | 61(1) | **Restricted** retail **without a qualified person** (non-C/C1/X) |
| Form 20B | 61(1) | **Wholesale**, non-C/C1/X |
| Form 21 | 61(2) | Retail of Schedule C & C(1) (excl. X) |
| Form 21A | 61(2) | Restricted retail, Schedule C(1) only, without qualified person |
| Form 21B | 61(2) | Wholesale of Schedule C & C(1) (excl. X) |
| Form 20F / 20G | 61(3) | Retail / wholesale of **Schedule X** |

- Applications: **Form 19** (fee ₹1,500), **Form 19A** (₹500), **Form 19C** for Schedule X — rule 59(2). Licensing authorities appointed by State Governments — 59(1).
- **Retail qualified person — rule 65(2):** supply on prescription only by or under personal supervision of a **registered pharmacist** (Pharmacy Act 1948 s.2(i)). Premises ≥ 10 sq m (≥ 15 sq m combined retail+wholesale), Schedule N compliance — rule 64(1). Display titles per rule 65(15).
- **Wholesale — rule 64(2) proviso** (NOT rule 63; current rule 63 is "Duration of licence" — perpetual validity subject to 5-yearly retention fee): premises in charge of a **competent person** (registered pharmacist; or matriculate with 4 years' drug experience; or degree holder with 1 year's experience).
- Licence conditions (Form 20): display licence; report qualified-staff change **within one month**; purchase only against cash/credit memos from licensed dealers; Inspection Book **Form 35** (65(16)); compliance inspection at least every 3 years (65B).

## 5. Records & computerisation

- Prescription register 65(3)(1); H1 register 65(3)(h); OTC retail records 65(4)(1)–(3) (carbon copies retained); retail purchase records 65(4)(4) — supplier licence number, batch, manufacturer, serially numbered chronological bills; wholesale sale memos 65(5)(1), carbon copies 3 years (65(5)(2)).
- General retention **2 years** (65(7)); produce to Inspector on demand (65(6)); no duplicate recording when kept under another law (65(8)).
- **⚠️ The claim "computerised records require rule 65(2)(b) monthly signed printouts in bound volumes" COULD NOT BE VERIFIED** — the words printout/bound/computer appear nowhere in rule 65 of the official consolidated text. Likely state practice or industry guidance. **Needs professional review.** (PPR 2015 reg. 6.2(c) encourages computerising prescription records for retrieval.)

## 6. Expired drugs

- **Rule 65(17):** no drug may be **sold or stocked after its expiry date**. Proviso: drugs pending withdrawal/reimbursement/disposal action with the manufacturer may be stocked if **stored separately** from trade stock in packages prominently displaying **"Not for sale"**. Applies to all sale licences (retail and wholesale).
- Rule 21 is **not** an expired-drugs rule (it defines import terms) — the operative provision is 65(17). Post-cancellation disposal needs licensing-authority permission (66A).
- Disposal route (secondary source, needs professional review for retail applicability): Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016 Schedule I (yellow) — return to manufacturer/supplier or incineration; cytotoxics incinerated >1200°C. Commercially, expiry returns to supplier are contractual.

## 7. DPCO 2013 (pricing) — what a pharmacy ERP must respect

Source: NPPA consolidated DPCO 2013 (paragraph numbers per consolidated text; verify against gazette for legal filings).

- **MRP printing — paras 24(2) (scheduled) / 25(1) (non-scheduled):** manufacturer prints MRP "inclusive of all taxes" on label. **NOT D&C rule 96** (rule 96 is labelling of contents — no MRP requirement).
- **Retailer obligations:**
  - **Para 24(4)/25(3):** display the manufacturer's price list conspicuously on premises.
  - **Para 26:** sell no higher than **current price list or label MRP, whichever is less**.
  - **Para 27:** no loose/split sale above **pro-rata** price.
  - **Para 14(1):** scheduled formulations — no sale above **ceiling price**; overcharge recoverable with interest (14(2)); MRP revision within **45 days** of ceiling notification (13(1) proviso).
- **16% retailer margin** is built into the ceiling-price formula — para 4(1): P(c) = P(s) × (1 + 16/100).
- **10% annual cap** on MRP increases for non-scheduled formulations — **para 20** (excess recoverable with interest + penalty, 20(2)).

## Corrections to earlier assumptions (verified)

1. Schedule H1 register is **rule 65(3)(h)** (3-year retention); rule 65(11A) is no-substitution.
2. Wholesale qualified-person conditions are **rule 64(2) proviso**; rule 63 is licence duration.
3. **No central computer-records provision exists** — "65(2)(b) printouts" is unverified; needs professional review.
4. Expired drugs = **rule 65(17)**, not rule 21.
5. MRP printing = **DPCO paras 24/25**, not D&C rule 96.
